The gist of this game app is that this is going to be the same as the google chrome out-of-internet dinosaur game.
The difference will be the art, and selecting a dinosaur.
The tyranosaur is the same, runs, jumps.
The Triceratops can eat the cacti but not the rocks, instead of jumping, it has a cacti meter that allows to charge into rocks. while charging, it cannot consume cacti but is not hurt either by them. Going directly into a rock without charging causes a game over
The pterodactyl is the same as the tyranosaur but it keeps to the top of the screen. "Jumping" causes it to dive dodging aerial obstacles but being vulnerable to ground obstacles.
